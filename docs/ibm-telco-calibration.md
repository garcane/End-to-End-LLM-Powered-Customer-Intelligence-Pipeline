# IBM Telco calibration report

Generated by `python -m src.generators.calibrate`. This is the evidence base for
the member dimension, and for the three churn-model coefficients that are estimated
from it rather than asserted.

- **Source:** `IBM Source Files`
- **Rows measured:** 7,043

> Calibrated from the extended IBM Cognos sample (7,043 customers) in 'IBM Source Files'.

> Satisfaction Score and Churn Score are post-hoc annotations and are excluded from the analogue model; only their marginal distributions are used.

> Tenure is censored at 72 months in the source, so the membership tenure tail is extended beyond what the seed can evidence.

> Payment method is taken from the original workbook: the extended workbook relabels the four original categories into three and folds manual 'Electronic check' customers into 'Bank Withdrawal', which would overstate the automatic-payment share as 95.6% instead of 43.5%.

> Location, population, gender, marital status and dependants are deliberately not carried over: US geography, and sensitive attributes of no analytical value to a membership model.

---

## 1. Leakage audit

The extended IBM sample contains two columns that were assigned **after** the
churn outcome was known. Using either as a predictor would produce a model with
near-perfect accuracy and no real-world value, so both are excluded from the
analogue fit. Only their marginal distributions are carried over.

| Column | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Satisfaction Score` | **leaked** | 4 value groups covering 4,378 rows have a churn rate of exactly 0 or exactly 1 |
| `Churn Score` | **leaked** | 6 value groups covering 4,221 rows have a churn rate of exactly 0 or exactly 1; classes overlap by only 15 points on a 5-96 scale |

## 2. Tenure

- Mean **32.39** months, sd **24.54**
- Censored at **72 months**: the source covers a fixed observation
  window, so the true tenure tail is unobservable.
- Deciles: 1, 2, 6, 12, 20, 29, 40, 50, 60, 69, 72

The generator bootstraps from this empirical distribution rather than fitting a
parametric curve, then stretches it by 1.6x because annual membership terms are
not month-to-month telco contracts, and extends the tail beyond the censoring
point for legacy members.

working years, whereas the seed is a general consumer base. It is the single

IBM's `CLTV` is a **bounded index** (2,003-6,500), not a currency amount, so its
scale and spread are not transferred. `model_predictions.predicted_lifetime_value`
is a genuine revenue figure -- annual fee x expected remaining years x contribution
margin -- and is deliberately more dispersed than the index. Only the direction of
the churned/retained ratio carries over.

### A trap in the extended workbook

The extended sample relabels the original four payment methods into three, and the
relabelling is **lossy**. Every manual `Electronic check` customer -- a third of the
sample -- is folded into `Bank Withdrawal` alongside genuine automatic payers:

```text
original                      ->  extended
Bank transfer (automatic) 21.9%    Bank Withdrawal 55.5%
Electronic check          33.6%  /
Credit card (automatic)   21.6%    Credit Card     39.0%
Mailed check              22.9%  /  Mailed Check     5.5%
```

Taken at face value the extended labels put the automatic share at **95.6%** rather
than **43.5%**, and mis-specify the predictor that maps onto `auto_renewal`. The
calibration therefore reads payment method from the original workbook, where the
`(automatic)` marker survives. Correcting this moved the fitted coefficient from
-0.43 to **-0.53**, which is a near-exact match for the -0.50 published in
`Data Recomendations.md`.

## 5. Advocacy

- **45.8%** of customers referred at least one other customer.
- Among those who did, the mean was **4.27** referrals (max 11).
- Churned customers made **0.21x** as many referrals as retained ones.

That last figure is one of the strongest signals in the seed, and it has no
counterpart anywhere in the original table list. It is why the generator adds a
`member_referrals` table and a `no_recent_advocacy` term to the churn model.

## 7. Churn reasons

| IBM category | Share of churners |
| --- | ---: |
| Competitor | 45.0% |
| Attitude | 16.8% |
| Dissatisfaction | 16.2% |
| Price | 11.3% |
| Other | 10.7% |

The generator adopts the **two-level structure** (category then specific reason)
but not these weights: a professional body loses far fewer members to a direct
competitor than a telco does, and far more to cost and non-use. The membership
weights are a documented domain judgement in `config.CHURN_CATEGORIES`.

Mean tenure at churn varies by only **2.2 months** across the five
categories, so the generator keeps reason only weakly conditional on behaviour
rather than making it a strong function of it.

## 8. Analogue churn model

Fitted on the comparable sub-population: customers with internet service
(n = 5,517, churn rate 31.8%). Phone-only customers are excluded
because they hold a structurally different product; including them inverts the sign
on service breadth, since a cheap single-product customer is not the same thing as a
disengaged one.

In-sample AUC **0.789**.
